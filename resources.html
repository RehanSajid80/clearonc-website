The Hidden Cost Crisis

Why cancer misdiagnosis is quietly draining self-insured health plans, and what to do about it.

May 28, 2026 60 min

Cancer is now the #1 driver of high-cost claims for self-insured employers — but the real crisis is the treatment plans that should never have been written in the first place. Up to 40% of initial diagnoses change after expert review.

Three voices — a Moffitt physician executive, a benefits-strategy leader, and a board-certified patient advocate — unpack where the money is leaking, why traditional UM tools miss it, and how employers are operationalizing expert review without changing carriers.

Featuring Dr. Kamal Jethwani (Moffitt) · Nisha Gandhi (ClearOnc) · Dr. Grace Cordovano (CancerX)
